# Open items

Placeholders, what's built and what isn't, planned work and known issues.
Priorities are in the Game Plan doc, "Game modes". Refreshed 2026-10-27 (unattended QA sweep,
`docs/autonomy-log.md`).

- **Art, fighters:** all ten fighters, Cairn and the training dummy are generated rigs
  (`tools/gen_<id>.py`, drawn through `tools/splat3d.py`, `shade2d.py` or their own
  rasterisers). The whole roster is at **2x density** (route A, Oct 2026, PRs #237-#248: 192 or
  256 px frames, packed sheets `tools/pack_sheets.py` keep a 4v4 at about 160 MB of texture).
  The training dummy is still at 1x. The rigged fighters gained real detail at 2x; the painted
  ones (Barkley, Gillbert, Craglash) only crisper edges: real detail there means redrawn shapes,
  which is art work. PixelLab regeneration was tried and rejected (smeared, inconsistent frames;
  `docs/pixellab-spec.md`, branch `crease-2x-spike` kept unmerged). An artist is making new
  characters and movesets; the delivery format is still to be agreed; spec for drop-in art:
  `docs/SPRITES.md`.
- **Art, arenas:** all six maps are tiled from bought Szadi packs with raised walls, sunken
  water and scatter (#234) and hand-dressed (Twin Keeps pilot, then #254-#258 for the other
  five; dressing scenes in `assets/arenas/<id>_dressing.tscn`, galleries `docs/maps/`). Only
  the effects sheet and a few overlays are still programmer art (`tools/gen_sprites.py`).
  Rulings open for Kevin: margin / hoarding props (every map named a candidate in its PR),
  a second buildings pack on one map (Greenhouse windmill sacks, Quarry tools). Szadi's written
  OK for the cuts in a public repository is still pending (`LICENSES.md`).
- **Art, Arcade endings:** the eleven ending cards' pictures (`assets/ui/endings/<fighter>.png`, 256 x 144) are
  placeholders from `tools/gen_ending_cards.py` (the fighter's victory pose on a plain backdrop with the title). Real
  art goes at the same path and size (or a new `art` path in `data/endings/<fighter>.json`); the card's words in
  those files are a first draft for Kevin to edit.
- **Progression, known and left as is** (the QC of 2026-10-28, `docs/autonomy-log-2.md`): an idle second pad in
  "Versus: 2 players" pays player 1 a full-rate win and feeds the flawless and combo challenges (WONTFIX: rewards are
  cosmetic, and requiring both players to press would punish a real but passive partner; revisit if a leaderboard ever
  reads XP). Online, challenges count `online.events`, which can include predicted frames later rolled back, as
  MatchStats has since online shipped (WONTFIX here: counting confirmed frames only belongs to the netcode). For Kevin's
  playtest: the first Normal win pays about +575 XP (seven first-time challenges, level 1 to 3 in one fight), and the
  streak's +10% is silent on same-day fights (the row shows when the streak grows). Settings saved since PR #279 hold
  `game/progression=false` (it was written as the default), so those installs stay Off until it is switched On once.

- **Fixed (Oct 28): ambush openers into the lights.** Burrow and Pickpocket didn't combo into
  the assassins' lights (found writing the ambush trials, #296): Digby's first two lights are
  `no_flinch`, so the target Burrow launched landed into a knockdown before light_2, and
  Pickpocket's 28 hitstun ran out one tick before Pete's first jab landed. Kevin's ruling R10:
  creature key `ambush_flinch` (Digby, Pete) makes his jabs stagger while the ambush window is
  open (`ambush_t`, 90 ticks), and Pickpocket's hitstun went 28 to 30. Burrow > light, light,
  light and Burrow > light, light > heavy are now 4-hit combos; Tiptoe > Pickpocket > three
  lights too. Burrow > three lights > heavy still ends at the third light: three juggle moves
  use up `JUGGLE_MAX`, so the heavy whiffs on the airborne foe. Digby's CPU route
  `skill_2, light, light, light, heavy` would be a real combo as `skill_2, light, light, heavy`
  (left for Kevin). Burrow's 16 recovery ticks leave one tick to spare for the first light
  (a buffered press makes it).
