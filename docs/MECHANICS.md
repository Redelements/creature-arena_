# Ready 4 Rambles: mechanics reference

Every data key the simulation reads from `data/creatures/*.json` and `data/rules/*.json`, and the rules built on them.
The code in `src/sim/` is the source of truth; this page was checked against it (2026-10-14, keys, events and tick order again on 2026-10-27 at NET_VERSION 58).
`src/sim/` is deterministic: no randomness, no clock, no nodes, no `sin`/`cos`/`atan2`/`pow`/`exp`.

Abbreviations: **cs** = `src/sim/creature_sim.gd`, **ms** = `src/sim/match_sim.gd`, **tg** = `src/sim/terrain_grid.gd`. Times are ticks (60 a second); distances are logical pixels.

## How a tick runs

`MatchSim.step(inputs)` (ms), in order:

1. `events.clear()`, `tick += 1`.
2. `ko_freeze > 0`: count down, only buffer presses (`buffer_input`), return.
3. `slowmo > 0`: on odd ticks only buffer presses, return.
4. `hitstop > 0`: count down, buffer presses, return. (Match-wide hitstop is only set while `freeze_all`: two on the field.)
5. `terrain.tick(cell_occupied)`: damaged cells mend a step when their timer runs out (it walks only the cells that differ from the base, `_damaged`, found again whenever `version` has moved; a wall waits a tick under a solid body).
5b. The `surge_every` mutator (Surge rush): on a tick that's a multiple of it, every meter fills (`gain_energy`).
6. `CreatureSim.step()` per creature, unless its own `hitstop > 0` (a crowded field, ms:`impact_stop`): then it counts down and only buffers presses while the rest of the match goes on. Otherwise: buffer presses, count down timers and cooldowns, the Paper Stock regrow (`_regrow_sheets`), `exhaust_slow` and `regen_wait`, regen stamina (`stamina_regen` × `boost_regen`), `_tick_status` (taunt drag, statuses, poison, Stoneskin), breakaway check, the state handler (`_update_free` / `_update_attack` / `_update_dodge` / `_update_guard` / stun / landing / falling), then `_reel_step`, `_flip_step`, `_apply_knockback`.
6b. `_step_swallows()`: a swallowed creature rides inside its swallower; when its time is up it is pushed out behind (`_poop`).
7. `_step_projectiles()`: move, crack trail, hit or burst.
8. `_step_zones()`: wear off, slow foes standing in them.
8a. `_step_storms()`: lob storms pulse, then the last hit and the crack ring (see `projectile.storm`).
8b. `Plants.step()` (only while a seed's plant stands; see "Plants" under Terrain and zones): sprouts root a foe treading on them and grow into trees, trees bear an apple and hand it to the first fighter who touches it.
9. `_resolve_hits()`: every active hit segment against every foe, through `_apply_hit`; a segment also strikes the other side's sprouts it covers (`Plants.strike`, once per segment).
9b. `Hazards.step()` (matches with a `hazards` mutator only; see Arena hazards below): puddles dry, waves warn / sweep / flood, meteor showers plan and land, boulders warn / get sent back / roll / hit, faults crack / open / close, cores are picked up.
10. `_separate()`: push solid bodies apart (an armored body doesn't budge, nor one in its own hitstop); then `_unstick()` pushes any solid body out of a wall or fence it overlaps.
11. `_check_falls()`: anyone over a hole starts falling (not while in its own hitstop).
11b. `_step_bridges()` (maps with `bridges` only): this tick's heavy hits crack the bridge under whoever they hit; a bridge strained enough falls in, a fallen one counts down to standing again.
12. Training only (`training` and a flag on): `_apply_training()` (see Training options below).

`DuelSim.step` and `TeamSim.step` wrap this with phases, timers and win checks.

The arena intro (main `_maybe_intro`, `src/view/arena_intro.gd`) is not part of the sim: while it plays main doesn't call `step` at all, so the match's first tick, its replay frames and every later tick are the same with or without it (`tests/test_ui.gd`). It plays only offline; `--intro` / `--no-intro` force it on or off (default off headless and for command-line starts).

Time of day and weather (`data/weather.json`, `src/view/weather_view.gd`) are views only: the sim never reads them, so a match's digest is the same in every look (`tests/test_weather.gd`). An arena's optional `times` / `weathers` lists (in `data/arenas/<id>.json`, so in the online data hash) say which presets it allows. Offline matches take the Single player menu's Time / Weather (Random within the arena's list by default) or `--time=random|day|sunset|night` / `--weather=random|clear|rain|fog|wind|snow` for the session; online matches, spectating and the menu's background fight are always day + clear; a replay records its look in `setup.look` (cosmetic) and an old replay without one plays day + clear.

## Move keys

A move's type comes from `type`, or its id prefix (`light*`, `heavy*`, `surge*`, else `skill`) in cs:`_normalize_move`. **Seg** means a `hits[]` entry may override the value; **chg** means a `<key>_charged` value is blended in by `charge_ratio` (cs:`move_value`; only when the segment doesn't override it).

## Combo and cancel rules

Constants: cs (`BUFFER_TICKS` 10, `JUGGLE_MAX` 3, `JUGGLE_FLOAT` 16, `LAND_KNOCKDOWN` 24, `GETUP_INVULN` 12, `BREAKAWAY_COST` 50, `BREAKAWAY_MIN_HITS` 3, `BREAKAWAY_INVULN` 30, `WALL_BOUNCE_MIN` 2.0, `WALL_BOUNCE_STUN` 12, `TAUNT_SPEED` 0.6, `TAUNT_PULL` 0.6, `STRAFE_SPEED` 0.85, `MAX_ENERGY` 100, `FRICTION` 0.82, `AIR_FRICTION` 0.92); ms (`HITSTUN_DECAY` 0.07, `HITSTUN_FLOOR` 0.4, `STALE_MULT` 0.5, `ENERGY_PER_DAMAGE` 1.5 and `ENERGY_PER_DAMAGE_TAKEN` 1.0 (defaults; `data/rules/energy.json` sets 0.8 and 0.5, plus `per_type_mult` and `variety_energy`), `STONE_SHATTER_MULT` 1.25, `BLOCKED_ENDER_DISADVANTAGE` 16); sim_defs (`TYPE_RANK` light 1, heavy 2, skill 3, surge 4; `VARIETY` [1.0, 1.0, 1.15, 1.3, 1.5]).

- **Buffer:** a press is kept `BUFFER_TICKS`, including presses during hitstop, KO freeze and slow motion. A Dodge press is kept (`dodge_buffer`) when it can't happen yet: during hitstop, the dodge cooldown, or an attack it can't cancel (below); pressed during an attack it waits until the attack ends, then `BUFFER_TICKS` more. A kept press from before an attack is dropped when that attack starts raw (cs:`_try_start_buffered`): only a press during the attack may cancel it.
- **Dodge cancel:** Dodge during your own attack leaves it at any point (windup, charge, startup, active or recovery: cs:`_update_attack` → `can_dodge_cancel`, `cancel_attack`, `_start_dodge`) for `dodge_cancel_cost` (the dodge's cost × `dodge_cancel_cost_mult`). Not while a blocked ender is being served (`punish_serial`), rooted, on the dodge cooldown, or short of that stamina. The cancelled move lands no more hits; a projectile already thrown flies on. A Dodge pressed in the hitstop of your own hit cancels on the first tick after it.
- **String cancel:** a buffered light during a light from `cancel_from` starts `next` (no hit needed), re-aimed where you hold.
- **Rank cancel:** once the move `connected` (hit, blocked or armored), any usable move of a higher rank from `rank_cancel_from`.
- **String branch:** a move's `branches` swaps which move a button starts at that point (the rank-cancel rules still apply; a branch that can't start falls back to the usual move).
- **Release:** from `armor.release_from` or `gaze.release_from` to the window's end, any other usable move (no hit or rank needed).
- **Windup** is added only on raw starts (cs:`_try_start_buffered`). During it the move doesn't move or steer (a charge move keeps charging and aiming), armor, leap and ghost are off, and any hit cancels it.
- **Steering:** until `aim_frames` and while not connected, the held aim (64-step) re-faces the move; a taunted fighter aims at its taunter.
- **Blocked ender:** a blocked `light_3` or later sets `punish_serial` (no rank cancel; the string cancel still works) and adds `block_lag` so the attacker recovers at least `BLOCKED_ENDER_DISADVANTAGE` ticks after the blocker (`punishable` event).
- **Damage:** `damage × atk_mult (steal) × vuln_mult × ambush`, then armor or guard; on a clean hit `× STONE_SHATTER_MULT` if a statue, `(+ stack bonus) × variety × stale`. Stale (×0.5) means the same move id already landed in this combo with another attack serial.
- **Hitstun:** `hitstun × max(HITSTUN_FLOOR, 1 − HITSTUN_DECAY × (hit# − 1)) × stale`.
- **Reaction precedence** (first that applies): no_flinch → petrify/freeze → grab+swallow → grab+flip → grab+pull (hook) → plain grab (placed behind, knockdown) → knockdown → launch → juggle float (if airborne) → crumple → guard-break crumple → hitstun. Then pull and reel.
- **Juggles:** airborne targets take only `juggle` non-grab hits from at most `JUGGLE_MAX` attacks. Landing: `LAND_KNOCKDOWN`, then `GETUP_INVULN`.
- **Wall bounce:** knockback ≥ `WALL_BOUNCE_MIN` bounces (×0.6); the first per combo adds `WALL_BOUNCE_STUN`.
- **Guard:** guarding turns to the nearest foe; a hit within `dot > 0.3` of facing is blocked: no health lost (no chip) and no meter for the attacker, stamina drain (`MatchSim.block_drain`: damage × `drain_per_damage` × `block_drain_mult[type]`), regen paused `block_regen_pause_seconds` (restarted by every blocked hit), knockback ×0.4, stun `hitstun × 0.5`. A blocked hit whose drain would take stamina to 0 isn't blocked: stamina goes to 0, the guard drops (`drop_guard`, a `guard_break` event with `drained`) and the hit lands clean, with only its own reaction. Hits from behind are clean.
- **Stamina** (`data/rules/stamina.json`, `CreatureSim.stamina_rules`; `block_drain_mult` light 0.75, heavy 1.25, skill 1.0, surge 1.0; `block_regen_pause_seconds` 0.5, `exhaust_delay_seconds` 3, `exhaust_speed_mult` 0.8, `exhaust_slow_seconds` 1.5; `dodge_cost_share_by_fighter` digby 0.4, pete 0.4, gillbert 0.45, hissy 0.5, craglash 0.7): at 0 from any source (`spend_stamina`: a dodge, a dodge cancel or a blocked hit) the creature is exhausted (`is_exhausted`): `can_guard` is false, so a held Guard does nothing, and `regen_wait` is set to `exhaust_delay_seconds`, replacing a block pause rather than adding to it; `exhaust_slow` (a state field) is set to `exhaust_slow_seconds` and while it runs `walk_speed` is × `exhaust_speed_mult`. `regen_wait` counts down every tick, in any state.
- **Armor:** hits deal `× (1 − reduction)`, no stun or knockback, hitstop ×0.6; grabs ignore it; an armored body isn't pushed.
- **Energy** (`data/rules/energy.json`, `MatchSim.energy_rules()`, in `Netplay.data_hash`): the attacker gains damage dealt (clean or armor chip; a blocked hit deals none, so it gives none) × `per_damage_dealt` (0.8) × `per_type_mult[type]` (light 0.45, heavy 1.0, skill 1.0; a missing type 1.0) × `variety_energy[n]` (`[1.0, 1.0, 1.3, 1.6, 1.9]`, indexed by `n`, the move types the target's current combo has used counting this hit: `SimDefs.type_count(d.combo_types | bit)`, capped at the table's end; a missing table is 1.0), all in `MatchSim.energy_dealt`. Surge-type hits earn nothing. A clean hit's damage already carries the combo's `VARIETY` bonus, so the two stack. An armored hit counts the target's combo types too (usually none, so `n` is 1). Without `per_type_mult` and `variety_energy` every hit charges alike, as before Oct 2026 (C2 `combo-meter`). The defender gains `per_damage_taken` (0.5) × clean damage (no type or variety factor). So lights alone barely fill the meter and strings that mix types fill it fastest; tune with `tests/bench_cpu.gd --combat` (docs/plans/combat-metrics.md). `carry_between_rounds` (true): a best-of-three keeps each side's meter into the next round (`DuelSim._next_round`; offline Versus in `main.gd`, `_carried_energy`); health, stamina and the floor still reset. `surge_ready` fires when a meter fills.
- **Breakaway:** Guard + Dodge while stunned, with `combo_hits >= 3` and 50 energy: combo reset, `BREAKAWAY_INVULN`, pushed away; the attacker's attack is cancelled.
- **KO:** `ko_freeze_ticks` 18 frozen, then `ko_slowmo_ticks` 70 at half speed (ms:`emit_event`; not for the dummy, and not on a crowded field: `freeze_all` off).
- **Falls:** `FALL_TICKS` 45, `FALL_DAMAGE` 8, respawn at the nearest solid cell with floor all round it (`nearest_solid(pos, 1)`: not at a pit's lip; the closest walkable cell if none has room) with `RESPAWN_INVULN` 40.

## Status effects

**Slow** (`slow_ticks`, zones, gaze): walk × `slow_mult`; a new hit overwrites the multiplier, ticks take the max.

**Taunt** (Bedrock, Craglash's Surge): foes within `radius` walk at `TAUNT_SPEED`, are dragged `taunt.pull` px/tick (default `TAUNT_PULL`, saved per victim as `taunt_pull`) toward the taunter while idle, walking or guarding, and aim every attack at it. Ends if the taunter is KO'd.

**Vulnerable** (Tenderize): incoming hit damage × `vuln_mult` (armor chip included), poison not; a block still takes none.

**Poison** (Venom Spit): every `poison_every` ticks of what's left, `poison_damage` plus `poison_stacks × poison_stack_dmg` (Venom Frenzy) through `take_damage` (Stoneskin soaks it). Outside the combo system; it can KO. The stacks and `poison_dmg` clear when the poison runs out.

**Statue** (`petrify`, `freeze_ticks`): crumple for N ticks, even from the air; `stone` clears the moment the target leaves crumple. The next clean hit shatters it for `STONE_SHATTER_MULT` (`shatter` event, `ice` flag).

**Blind** (Headlamp Flash): the next attack the target *starts* misses with every hit, projectiles included (`miss` event); starting it uses the blind up. Waiting it out is the counterplay.

**Root** (Cave-In, Pinecone Bomb and its plants, Root Eruption): walk speed 0 (so `mobile` is 0) and no dodging. Attacking, lunges and dashes still move. A new root takes the max of what's left, never adds. Root Eruption's goes through a guard (`root_through_guard`).

**Stealth** (Tiptoe): drawn faint; the CPU chases the last place its team saw him (cpu_controller `_look`, `_seen`). Ends on any hit except a ranged `no_flinch` one (a thrown knife flick), or at the first active tick of one of his own attacks unless that move itself grants stealth. That attack's hits get `ambush`, and so does every hit for `ambush_ticks` after. A `hidden` move (Digby's Burrow) grants it from its own start and springs the ambush with its strike.

**Steal** (Pickpocket): overwritten, not stacked.

**Gaze** (Stone Gaze): every `every` ticks in `[from, to]`, each foe within `range` that is in front of her (`cone`) *and* facing her (`facing`) gets `slow_mult` lowered by `step` (floor `min_mult`) and slow kept for `slow_ticks`. Looking away, or getting behind her, is the counterplay.

- Grid: `GRID_COLS` 84 × `GRID_ROWS` 36 cells of `CELL` 8 px from (`GRID_X` 16, `GRID_Y` 64). Cells are `INTACT` 0, `CRACKED` 1, `HOLE` 2, and the arena map's fixed pieces `WALL` 3, `FENCE` 4, `CHASM` 5, `SHALLOWS` 6, `BRUSH` 7, `BRUSH_FLAT` 8.

Online matches pin the stadium.
- Sinkhole (`data/rules/sinkhole.json`, `TeamSim.sinkhole`): every `shrink_every` ticks ring `ring` of the floor is cracked, `sim.sink_ring` set and `sim.sink_safe` set to the cells that will be left (the CPU walks toward the centre when it stands outside `sink_safe` shrunk by a cell); `shrink_warn` ticks later `sink()` drops the ring into chasm for good (`base` too). `TeamSim.ring_rect(r)` is the floor (cells) left after rings 0..r: each ring takes `shrink_cols` cells off the left and right and `shrink_rows` off the top and bottom, and an axis stops at `min_cols` / `min_rows`; rings stop at `max_rings` or when nothing is left to take (today 14 rings, 7 s apart, ending on 28 × 16 cells). `fall_ko` on the match makes `_land_from_fall` a KO only when the fall was into a **chasm** (`CreatureSim.fall_chasm`, decided at `start_fall`, since a hole can heal during the fall): the Sinkhole ring, an arena's chasm pieces and `pit_floor`'s chasms. A creature's HOLE (Storm Drain, Slam) does the usual `FALL_DAMAGE` and puts you on the nearest solid floor. Events `sink_warn`, `sink` carry `ring` and the floor `rect` left after that ring (pixels). `tests/bench_sinkhole.gd` measures the pacing.
- Plants (`Plants` in `src/sim/plants.gd`; `MatchSim.plants`, `plant_seq`, in `snapshot()`, `restore()` and, once a seed has been planted, `state_digest()`): a lob with a projectile `seed` block (Pinecone Bomb) that bursts on nobody leaves a sprout where it fell, `{id, owner, team, pos, hp, hp_max, age, stage, apple, root, grow, apple_at, heal_share, radius, pick_radius}`. A **sprout** (`stage` 0) has `hp` (10): a strike segment, a shot or a burst of the other side that covers it takes the hit's raw `damage` off (once per segment or shot; a shot flies on), at 0 it's gone (`plant_gone` `killed`). A foe whose body touches it (on its feet: not airborne, down, falling, knocked out, mid-leap or in dodge i-frames) is rooted for `root` ticks (the throw's `root_ticks` × `root_share`: 45) and springs it (`plant_root`, `rooted`, `plant_gone` `sprung`); the owner's side walks over it. After `grow` ticks (35 s) it's a **tree** (`plant_grow`) with its `hp` back to full: the other side can cut it down the same way (apple and all), it roots nobody, and `apple_at` ticks (2 s) later it bears an apple (`apple`). The first fighter touching the tree (within `pick_radius` + body) takes it: one of the owner's side who is hurt heals `heal_share` (15%) of their own max HP (`apple_eaten`, power = hp healed; an ally at full health walks past), a foe spoils it (`apple_spoiled`); either way the tree is gone (`plant_gone` `eaten` / `spoiled`). Plants aren't solid, cast no hit of their own, and are cleared with the projectiles between Caravan rounds. The CPU doesn't know about them yet.
- King of the Crater (`data/rules/crater.json`, `TeamSim.crater`, on the deathmatch respawns): the zone (`sim.zone_pos`, `sim.zone_r` = `zone_radius` 40) cycles `zone_points` (floor fractions) every `zone_move_every` ticks, cracking a circle round its rim when it lands (`zone_move` event). `zone_holder()` is the side alone inside (down, falling and airborne fighters don't count); it scores a tick a tick, and every `crack_every` held ticks one rim cell takes a step of damage (`aim_dirs` index `k×11`). `score_to_win` is a plain tick count; KOs don't score. - Caravan (`data/rules/caravan.json`, `TeamSim.caravan`, on the deathmatch respawns; the rules' `arena` picks its map): the map's `track` (floor points from side 0's end to side 1's; `TeamSim.track_point`, `track_length`) carries the caravan for `round_seconds` (120) a round, `cv_p` px from the attackers' start (`cv_round` 0: side 0 attacks from the track's start; 1: side 1 from its end). Fighters within `radius` count (down and falling ones don't; airborne do). Attackers alone: `cv_p += push_speed × push_mult[attackers]`; both sides: it stays; defenders alone: `cv_p -= back_speed`, floored at the last checkpoint passed (`checkpoints`, shares of the track; each adds `checkpoint_seconds` to `timer`, event `checkpoint`). At `timer` ≤ 0 the round continues while an attacker is within `radius`, up to `overtime_seconds` (`cv_ot`). A finish records `cv_time[side]` (ticks); round 1 ends early once past `cv_best[0]` (round 0 unfinished) or at `cv_time[0]` ticks (round 0 finished). - Pieces: a wall stops bodies and shots (lobs fly over) and bounces knockback like the arena edge; a fence stops bodies only; a chasm is a pit that never heals; shallows walk at `SHALLOWS_WALK` 0.6, brush at `BRUSH_WALK` 0.85. A wall never rebuilds under a solid body (`tick(blocked)` waits a tick).
- Bodies vs pieces: `_move` slides along walls one axis at a time (`slide_x/y`); `_unstick` (after `_separate`) pushes any solid body out of a piece it overlaps (`resolve_body`: nudge off an edge, else the shortest straight way out). `nearest_solid` only returns walkable cells.
- Crossings: leaps and Burrow cross chasms (they ignore pits already; the CPU throws a move with a `leap` round a straight `dash` over a chasm in its way when it comes down on floor: `CpuController._gap_cross`, off for a fighter whose `ai.cross_pits` is false); ghost dashes and `passes` cross fences; `breaks` charges smash walls; any attack dash, homing dash or dodge flattens the brush under its body (`_trample`, `BRUSH_FLAT`, grows back). Brush sets `covered` on whoever stands in it (cell under the centre, after `_unstick`). Nothing in the sim reacts to it; who sees whom is `Vision` (below).
- **Vision** (`src/sim/vision.gd`, read-only: the sim never calls it): one rule for the screen and the CPU. A team sees what any living member sees (and everyone once none is standing). A viewer always sees itself, its teammates and KO'd fighters. Otherwise: **line of sight** first, a cell walk from centre to centre (`clear_line`, Amanatides-Woo, plain arithmetic; a corner crossing checks both cells beside it) that a `WALL` or `FENCE` cell blocks (the two end cells don't count; a smashed wall is cracked floor, so sight passes; chasms, holes, shallows and brush don't block). Then **brush**: a `covered` fighter is seen only within `HIDE_RANGE` 34 px, while it attacks (state ATTACK, from the first startup frame), while it reels from a hit (HITSTUN, CRUMPLE, AIRBORNE, KNOCKDOWN), or for `REVEAL_TICKS` 30 after it landed or took a hit (`hit`, `block`, `armor`, `grab`, `hook`, `guard_break`, `shatter` events, by their `tick`). There's no last-hit tick in sim state, so the owner feeds `observe(sim.events)` every tick (main: the events it shows; the CPU: in `bits`); same inputs, same answer on every machine. Stealth stays separate (drawn as a shimmer; the CPU counts it as out of sight).
- On screen (main `_viewer_team`, `_update_vision`): the viewer is the one team the players at this machine are on (online: the local slot's). A foe that team can't see isn't drawn at all: sprite, shadow, bar and label, aim mark, telegraphs, status icons, its particles and its own events (a hit it lands on someone seen still sparks; shots, bursts and zones show when their spot is in sight). The camera doesn't frame it. Where the team last saw it, a faint ring and "?" fade over a second. Your own and your allies' fighters in brush are drawn at 45% (unchanged). With no single local team (CPU vs CPU, replays, watching online, local players on both sides) everyone is shown, brush at 45%; `--view-team=N` shows such a match through team N's eyes.
- The CPU (`cpu_controller.gd:_look`): `Vision.team_sees` for its own team plus stealth; a foe out of sight is chased, aimed at and targeted by where the team last saw it (`_last_seen` per foe, `_seen`), and its swings aren't read (`_defend`, `_rage_defend`, `_blocked_and_open`). Known leak, not changed: the sim's own foe picks (`nearest_opponent`: guard auto-facing, homing, blinks) still find hidden foes. Aim assist no longer does: it bends toward `PlayerInput.assist_foe`, the nearest foe standing with `stealth` 0 (brush-`covered` foes still count), and the touch lock already skips every hidden foe.
- Caches the CPU and Vision share (exact answers, faster): `TerrainIndex` (summed-area counts of walls/fences, pits and chasms per terrain version) answers "anything solid in this box?" before a line or body check walks the cells; `Vision.clear_line` keeps its answers for the terrain's current version, since every CPU on a team and the screen ask the same lines in a tick; `NavGrid.shared(terrain)` is one nav grid for all CPUs, rebuilt only in the node rows whose cells changed, with regions (`_label_regions`) so a path between unjoined areas is empty without a search. Paths are A* with an octile estimate (cheapest cost, ties in a fixed order). main asks all CPU sides at once (`_think_cpus` -> `CpuController.think_all`, the worker threads; the answers wait in `last_bits`), the same inputs as asking one after another (`tests/test_cpu_pathing.gd`); `--cpu-threads=off` asks them one by one on the main thread. With a screen, main sets the CPUs going on the next tick's inputs at the end of a tick (`_think_ahead`, `CpuController.think_start`), so they think while the frame draws, and takes the answers only if the next tick steps the same match from the same tick (`tests/test_ui.gd` checks the inputs are unchanged). `--perf` logs each fight frame's wall time and each tick's cost (`PerfLog`) with the share of each part (tick steps, each view's draw, the renderer's CPU and GPU time) and prints percentiles on quit (also `user://perf.txt`, one log line each so Android's logcat keeps them): the 1% and 0.1% lows, missed frames and the slowest second, the game's time split into frames with a tick and without, and ticks whose picture stayed up 3+ frames. These are times on the CPU's side: on Android the frame queue hides them from the screen, so `tools/odin_perf.py` also reads what SurfaceFlinger presented. The report's setup line names the shader warm-up (`ShaderWarmup`: its materials, draws, the renderer's time for them and the slowest title frame while it ran; `warmup: ...` is also printed when it ends) and a line gives the first 120 fight frames the percentiles leave out (where a shader compile shows); `--no-warmup` skips the warm-up for an A/B. `--rematch-after=N` presses an offline match's Rematch by itself N frames into its results screen (back-to-back CPU fights for `tools/odin_perf.py --soak`, a 10-minute heat soak). `--fight-fps=N` sets this session's fight cap; `--fps-pacing=cap|vsync` picks the frame limiter or vsync for a cap at or over the screen's refresh rate (`main.gd:pace_cap`; vsync on mobile). `--cpu-think=threads|gap` picks where the next tick's CPU thinking runs at 120 fps and up: the worker threads, or the main thread in the frame between ticks (`_think_in_gap`, within `gap_budget_us`, the rest in the tick; gap on mobile, where the worker threads took the two fast cores from the game's thread). Same inputs either way.
- Each damaged cell steps back one level every `HEAL_TICKS` 720; fresh damage restarts its timer at `max(timer, heal)`.
- Walking, lunges, non-leap dashes, `mobile` and taunt drag refuse to step onto a hole; knockback, dodges, reels, flips and leaps don't.
- `_check_falls` skips FALLING, KO, DODGE, AIRBORNE, leaping and reeling fighters, and invulnerable ones unless knocked down.
- Projectile cracks (trail, lob `crack`) never open holes.
- Zones (`MatchSim.zones`, snapshotted): each tick a foe of the owner standing inside (not airborne, falling or KO) gets `slow_mult = min(current, zone)` and at least 8 ticks of slow, so the slow lingers briefly after stepping out.

## Team rules

- `CreatureSim.team` defaults to its `id` (1v1 = two teams of one); `TeamSim` sets it to the side; `DuelSim` gives every member id = side.
- `is_foe(o)`: not itself and a different team. Hits, projectiles, bursts, taunts, gazes and zones skip teammates; `nearest_opponent` (aim, homing, blink, guard facing, CPU) only returns foes. Bodies still push teammates apart.
- Duel (`data/rules/duel.json`, duel_sim.gd): one creature per side; `IN_TAG` swaps from neutral/guard, or out of a connected attack for `tag_cancel_energy` 50; the `tag_in` move starts with `startup + tag_in_invuln` invulnerability; energy and stamina carry over; benched members regen; 180 s, then health share wins. Keys: `team_size` (3; read by main.gd), `tag_cooldown` (300 ticks after a tag), `tag_in_invuln` (10), `tag_cancel_energy` (50), `bench_regen_per_second` (1.5 hp) up to its health when tagged out plus `bench_regen_cap` (0.25) of its max HP, `mid_ko_slowmo` (40, after a KO with members left), `ko_swap_ticks` (110: phase `SWAP`, then the next member enters with `enter_invuln` 60), `intro_ticks` (100).
- Online 1v1 (`data/rules/duel_1v1.json`): a Duel with one creature a side, best of three. `DuelSim.rules_for(teams)` / `rules_id_for` pick it (`"1v1"`, saved as the replay setup's `rules`; older replays have `""` and keep duel.json). Keys on top of duel.json's: `rounds_to_win` (2; 1 = no rounds), `round_over_ticks` (210, phase `ROUND_OVER`), `max_rounds` (5: then the side with more rounds wins, else a draw); `time_seconds` 90 per round. A drawn round counts for nobody. The next round restores the snapshot taken at construction (health, places, floor, clock) but keeps `sim.tick`, `round_wins`, `round_num` and `round_winner`, which are all in DuelSim's snapshot and digest. `round_over` fires only for a round that doesn't end the match; the deciding one sends `duel_over`.
- Team battle (`data/rules/team.json`): everyone on the field, one life each; 150 s, then health share.
- Keys every team rules file may set (TeamSim): `time_seconds`, `intro_ticks` (100), `mid_ko_slowmo` (a KO that doesn't end it: 30, deathmatch 18), `spawn_spread` (60 px between a side's starting spots; CTF 40), `pad_inset` (44 px from the side wall to the respawn pads; CTF 40) and `pad_spread` (48 px between pads; CTF 36; Caravan attackers also stack across the track by it). `over_ticks` (how long the result shows) is read by main.gd, not the sim.
- Team Deathmatch (`data/rules/deathmatch.json`, modes 31/32): `deathmatch` swaps one life for respawns after `respawn_seconds` on `pad_point(side, slot)` against each team's own wall; every KO adds to the other team's `score`; `guard_t` spawn protection lasts `spawn_protect_seconds` or until the fighter attacks; first to `score_to_win[per_side]` wins, else the higher score at time, and a tie goes to `overtime` sudden death (next KO wins). `score`, `respawn_t`, `guard_t` and the overtime flag are in TeamSim's snapshot. Main fills `FxView.pads` (the floor rings). Killing sprees (`spree_at` > 0, plain TDM only: not the Crater, Caravan or CTF, which also set `deathmatch`): KO credit as in the Brawl crown (`credit_seconds` 5, `MatchSim.note_credit` into TeamSim's `hit_by` / `hit_at`; a respawn clears it). When a slot goes down (`_spree_ko`), its `streak` (credited KOs since it last went down) resets; its credited killer, on the other team and standing, adds one to its own. `spree_level(slot)` = `streak - spree_at + 1` from `spree_at` (3) on, else 0; event `spree` when it rises. KOing a fighter with level L > 0 is a shutdown: the killer's team scores 1 + min(L × `shutdown_bonus` (1), `shutdown_cap` (3)), the killer gets `shutdown_energy` (40) Surge energy (`gain_energy`), event `shutdown`; a KO with nobody credited still scores the 1. The bonus counts toward `score_to_win` (a shutdown can win), and sudden death is still the next KO. Each tick `MatchSim.marks[slot]` = `spree_cpu_pull` (60) × min(level, `shutdown_cap`). `streak`, `hit_by`, `hit_at` are in TeamSim's snapshot and digest. Online too: the rules id rides in hello and `start` (`TeamSim.rules_by_id`).

## Training options

- `MatchSim.training` is set by `MatchFactory.training` while the match is being built (its setter refuses once a creature is in or a tick has run), so no versus, Duel, team, Brawl, online, Combo Trials or Reaction Drill match is ever a training match. It is fixed for the match (not snapshotted).
- In a training match nobody stays down: a knocked-out fighter, the dummy or the player (a meteor, a boulder or the dummy's own attacks can do it), gets back up at full health and stamina after `CreatureSim.DUMMY_RESET_TICKS` (90), and a knockout freezes nothing (`emit_event` skips `ko_freeze` / `slowmo` when `training`). Every other match keeps its knockout. (Oct 2026: before this a meteor KO left the player down for good with the shower hanging in the air, since hazards pause while fewer than two sides stand.)
- `MatchSim.training_flags` (`TRAINING_FLAGS`: `no_cooldowns`, `full_energy`; a missing key is off) is set only through `set_training_flags(flags)`, which returns false and changes nothing outside training and drops unknown keys. It is in `snapshot()`/`restore()`, and in `state_digest()` for training matches only (every other match's fingerprint is unchanged).
- After every full tick (step 12), for each creature that isn't the dummy: `no_cooldowns` sets every `cooldowns` entry (moves and the dodge) to 0, `stamina` to `max_stamina` and `regen_wait` to 0 (most dodges are gated by stamina; guard drain is refilled too); `full_energy` sets `energy` to `MAX_ENERGY` (no `surge_ready` event, so no chime after every Surge). A move used this tick is usable on the next.
- Replays: a training setup carries `training_flags` and `flag_changes` `[[frame, no_cooldowns, full_energy], ...]`; `ReplayBuilder.apply_flag_changes` applies them before that frame's inputs (`ReplayBuilder.play` and the Replay theater).
- Replay viewer (`src/game/replay_viewer.gd`, main's `replay_ctl`; outside the sim, the recorded frames never change): main runs `ticks_now()` ticks a physics frame while a replay is watched (0 paused, quarter-tick steps for 1/4x and 1/2x, 2 at 2x). A jump (`seek(T)`) restores the stepping object (the DuelSim / TeamSim / BrawlSim, else the MatchSim) in place from the nearest keyframe at or before T (`snapshot()` every 60 ticks, more apart past 480 of them) and steps the recorded frames headless to T, a Versus round's intro with no inputs as main plays it; main's Versus flow, clock and banners are put back from T (`sim.tick = INTRO_TICKS + fight ticks`). The same `state_digest()` as playing straight to T (`tests/test_replay_tools.gd`).
- `--bug-report[=DIR]` (CLI; `src/game/bug_report.gd`): a zip in DIR (default `user://bug-reports`) with `system.txt`, `logs/` (the two newest Godot logs, the player id and secret blanked), `settings.cfg` (without `[online]` id, secret, name, address, server, recent), `progress.json`, `replay/<file>`, `perf.txt`; prints `BUGREPORT <path>` and quits, or with `--quit-after=N` is written at the end of the run. File logging is on for every platform (`project.godot` `debug/file_logging/enable_file_logging`; it was on for desktops only).

## Input bits

One int per player per tick (src/sim/sim_defs.gd). Buttons: `IN_UP` 1, `IN_DOWN` 2, `IN_LEFT` 4, `IN_RIGHT` 8, `IN_LIGHT` 16, `IN_HEAVY` 32, `IN_DODGE` 64, `IN_GUARD` 128, `IN_SKILL1` 256, `IN_SKILL2` 512, `IN_SURGE` 1024, `IN_TAG` 2048 (Duel: tag; Capture the Flag: pass the flag). `IN_AIM` 4096 plus a 6-bit index at `AIM_SHIFT` 13; `IN_STRAFE` bit 19 (face the aim while walking at `STRAFE_SPEED`; needs `IN_AIM`); `IN_MOVE` bit 20 plus a 6-bit index at `MOVE_SHIFT` 21. Indexes are 64 steps into `AIM_INT` (integers / 10000, normalized with sqrt only). `aim_from_bits` gives the aim, else the move direction; `move_from_bits` gives the move angle, else the 8-way bits. Encoders: `aim_bits`, `move_bits`.

Touch (src/input/touch_pad.gd, `PlayerInput.Device.TOUCH`) makes these same bits, so nothing in the sim or the online protocol changes: the stick sends `move_bits` (64 steps), a tap sends the button's bit for one tick (aim assist at `ASSIST_DEG` 25 even with the stick held), a drag off Heavy / Skill 1 / Skill 2 / Surge sends the press on release with `IN_AIM` (nothing when released in the cancel zone) (kept for `AIM_HOLD` ticks, like the mouse), a charging move held still on its button for `HOLD_TICKS` presses then and holds its bit, Guard holds, Dodge adds the stick's last way. The touch target lock (src/input/touch_target.gd) is input-side too: a tap's press is aimed with `IN_AIM` at the locked foe for `AIM_HOLD` ticks, and a Light-tap chase sends `move_bits` along the CPU's `NavGrid` path, so it needs no sim support either. `--touch-demo[=left]` plays a recorded touch script (`src/game/touch_demo.gd`) into player 1's pad for overlay screenshots (training as Cairn unless `--mode` is given; `=left` mirrors the controls for the session).

- An arena's `tiles` block (`set`, `ground`, `grass`): a tiled arena's ground, read by `tools/build_tiles.gd` (which bakes `assets/arenas/<id>.tscn`) and `src/view/arena_view.gd` (which instances it and draws the breaking ground over it from `assets/tiles/<set>_overlay.tres`). View only; the sim lays the same `pieces` on the grid whether or not a map is tiled. In `data_hash` like the rest of the arena file.
- `sprite.ppu` (and a view block's own `ppu`): the sheet's density in texture pixels per world unit, 1 unless given (every older sheet), 2 for art made for the 32 px scale (`src/view/art_scale.gd`, `ArtScale`): such a sheet draws at half scale on half-unit positions, its `frame`, `origin` and `portrait` in its own pixels. View only; the sim never reads it, and no sim number changes with it (`docs/plans/32px-migration.md`, `tests/test_art_scale.gd`).
- A move's `fx.move_fx` (one entry or a list; an entry is a `res://assets/vfx/<name>.tres` path, or `{"path": ..., <keys>}` to set that move's own `on`, `at`, `offset` [x, y], `follow`, `flip_with_facing`, `loop`, `loop_ticks`, `scale`, `ticks_per_frame` over the resource's: `MoveFx.from_entry`; one cut serves many moves): hand-drawn effects for the move, `MoveFx` resources (`src/view/move_fx.gd`; frames cut from kiddolink's pack by `tools/cut_vfx.py`, resources built by `tools/build_vfx.gd`). Each says when it spawns (`on`: `strike` = the tick the sim's hitbox goes live, from the same `startup` / `hits[]` the sim uses, `start`, `tick` `at`, `hit`, `projectile`, `projectile_end`, `burst`: a lob landing, its `burst` event or the `storm` event of a lob that leaves a storm), its offset, whether it follows the fighter, mirrors with the facing, loops. Played by `MoveFxView` under `FxView`, its frame set from the sim tick. View only: the sim never reads it; in `data_hash` like the rest of the creature file, no `NET_VERSION` bump needed for it.
- `data/voice.json` (proximity voice chat: codec rate and frame, the hearing radii, the teammates' floor, the gate, the jitter wait) is read by `src/net/voice_chat.gd` and `src/view/voice_view.gd` only; it isn't in `data_hash`, since nothing in it touches the sim. The flags `--voice=off|ptt|open` (this session's mode) and `--voice-test=tone` (a tone for the microphone, with a log line a second) are in `src/game/CLAUDE.md`.
- `data/endings/<fighter>.json` (Arcade ending cards: `title`, `lines`, `art`, optional `unlock_title`) is read by `src/game/endings.gd` and `src/ui/ending_screen.gd` only, with Settings > Game > Progression on; not in `data_hash`. Shown after an offline Arcade clear before the results (`main.gd:_open_ending`), seen state in progress.json's `endings`. `--menu=profile:endings` opens the Profile's Endings gallery and `--menu=ending:<fighter>` one card (both need Progression on).


Pitfalls found while checking:

- `hit_params` fills every hit key (0 when the move leaves it out), so a default in `hit.get(key, default)` never applies. `poison_every` and `reel_speed` are handled explicitly (`POISON_EVERY_DEFAULT` = 30, `REEL_SPEED_DEFAULT` = 5.0 in ms:`_apply_hit`); apply any new default the same way.
- `taunt` only fires on a move that also has `armor` (cs:`_update_attack`), once, at `armor.from`: foes who walk into the radius later aren't taunted.
- Move-level-only keys (`cone`, `slow_mult`, `vuln_mult`, `steal`) have no `_charged` blend.
- No current move reaches the plain-grab branch (ms:`_apply_hit`); the grabs are a hook, a flip or a swallow.
- Input: the League/mouse layout and the gamepad pass Heavy, Skill 1 and Skill 2 on while held (`MouseOrders.HOLD_BITS`), so any move that charges can be held on any layout. Until 2026-10-14 only Heavy was, so a held Gulp could only be done on the classic keys.
- Accessibility: `data/accessibility.json` is read by `src/view/accessibility.gd` only (views; not in `data_hash`): `teams` (`default` / `colour_safe`: `sides`, `brawl`, `self`, `ally`, `enemy`, `neutral`), `outlines` (`team` / `high_contrast`: `outer`, `inner`, `alpha`), `hit_markers` (`default` / `high_contrast`: `spark_scale`, `spark_width_add`, `spark_outline`, `spark_outline_width`, `number_size_add`, `number_outline_add`, `number_shadow`, `number_shadow_color`, `full_flash`). Settings > Accessibility picks them (saved under `[accessibility]`: `teams`, `outlines`, `markers`, all 0 by default); `--a11y=teams,outlines,markers` (or `outlines=team`, `all`) sets them for the session, not saved (`src/game/CLAUDE.md`).

## Where the bullet in CLAUDE.md was wrong or stale

1. **Taunt pull**: `taunt.pull` used to be dead data; it is now read (Bedrock's 1.6 px/tick) and `TAUNT_PULL` is only the default.
2. **`terrain_heal` "any terrain move"** (CLAUDE.md:164–165): it scales only `terrain_radius`/`terrain_rect` (cs:`_slam`, 1042) and `terrain_ring` (cs:853). `terrain_crack` (cs:857), projectile crack trails (ms:578) and lob cracks (ms:611) always use `HEAL_TICKS`.
3. **"A blocked third light can't cancel"** (CLAUDE.md:146–148): only the rank cancel is blocked (cs:919 checks `punish_serial`). The string cancel to the next light still works (cs:909–915; ms:467–469 says so), and so do armor/gaze releases. It applies to `light_3` *or later* (ms:472), and the gap is "at least" 16 ticks (ms:476).
4. **"Raw windup moves stay committed"** (CLAUDE.md:187–188): steering depends on move type, not on rawness. Every non-light without `aim_frames` gets 0 steering ticks, raw or cancelled into (cs:818). A cancelled-into big move "goes where you hold" only because the cancel re-faces it once (cs:921–922). Also the code comment at cs:815 ("8-way, like walking") is stale: steering uses the 64-step aim (cs:559). Only Crease's Paper Frog sets `aim_frames` (4).
5. **`slow_ticks`, `juggle_float` listed with Craglash's batch** (CLAUDE.md:119–120): no Craglash move uses either now. `slow_ticks` is on gillbert heavy and sno; `juggle_float` is on barkley, cerebro, crease, gillbert and hissy.
6. **"`no_flinch` hits only deal damage"** (CLAUDE.md:148–149): there is still no stun and no knockback, but statuses, `pull`, hitstop and energy gain all still apply (ms:308–362, 410–424).
7. **`hold` "overrides any hit value"; "flip, the swallow"** (CLAUDE.md:158–161): `hold` replaces only the `hit_params` dictionary (cs:347–349). A held Gulp now goes through its own `swallow` branch (ms:`_swallow`), not `_flip`.
8. **"Root: no walking or dodging"** (CLAUDE.md:134): correct, but dashes and lunges still move a rooted fighter (they use `_move`, not `walk_speed`); `mobile` stops (cs:831).
9. **"Dodge isn't buffered during attacks"** (CLAUDE.md:233–234): no longer true (October 2026, issue #9). A Dodge during your own attack cancels it (`can_dodge_cancel`, dearer: `dodge_cancel_cost_mult`); when it can't, the press is kept until the attack ends and `BUFFER_TICKS` after. See "Dodge cancel" above.
10. **"`gain_energy()` is the only way energy goes up"** (CLAUDE.md:168–169): within the sim, DuelSim also assigns energy on tag and on entry (duel_sim.gd:153, 216). That is a carry-over and emits no `surge_ready`. Trials and showcases set it directly (src/game).
11. **"Sno Mercy added `cone`"** (CLAUDE.md:141): accurate as history, but Culvert's Runoff also uses `cone` (0.45), and `cone` has no effect on projectiles (ms:`_step_projectiles` doesn't check it).
