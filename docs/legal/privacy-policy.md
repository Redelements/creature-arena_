**Who:** Ready 4 Rambles is made and operated by [Kevin Winstead / business name], [country].
Contact: [email].

**What the game stores on your device.** Settings, your progress (levels, unlocks, challenge
counts, local best scores), saved replays, and a log file for diagnosing problems. If you choose
"Save a bug report", the game writes a zip of those files for you to send us; it leaves out your
online player id and secret. Nothing on your device is sent anywhere unless you play online or
send that zip yourself.

**What is sent when you play online.** Connecting to a game server sends: your chosen display
name, your fighter and inputs during a match, the game version, and, for ranked matches and the
Daily Challenge only, a random player id and a random secret the game made on first use. These
identify your copy of the game; they are not linked to your name, email or account anywhere
else. Voice chat, if you turn it on, sends your microphone audio to the other players in your
match through the server, which relays it and does not record it.

**What the server keeps.** For ranked play and the Daily Challenge: your player id, a hash of
the secret (never the secret), your display name, your rating, and your Daily scores for the
last 60 days. The server may keep replays of recent online matches (inputs and names, no audio)
for a limited time to check results and find bugs, and connection logs (including IP addresses)
for a short period to keep the service running and block abuse. [State the retention: e.g.
logs 14 days, replays 30 days.]

**What we do not do.** No advertising, no analytics or tracking services, no selling or sharing
of data with anyone. We don't collect email addresses, payment details (the store handles
purchases) or anything about you beyond the list above.

**Third parties.** The game is sold through [Steam / Google Play]; their own privacy policies
cover what they collect. If Discord Rich Presence is enabled in the game, your Discord app
shows what you are playing; that goes through Discord's software on your machine, not our
server.

**Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13 [adjust per rating and region], and
we knowingly collect no personal information from them.

**Your rights.** You can delete everything on your device by deleting the game's user folder
([path]). To have your online record (id, name, rating, scores) deleted, email us the player id
shown in Settings > Game > Online; we remove it within [30] days. EU and UK players have the
rights given by the GDPR, including access and erasure; the legal basis for the processing above
is our legitimate interest in running the online service you asked to join.

**Changes.** We'll date any change to this policy here and mention it in the game's release
notes.
