### Part 3: 32 px leftovers

Details: `docs/qa/part3a-leftovers.md`, `docs/qa/part3b-shapes.md` (+ `tools/qa_shapes.py`).

- **minor** (3a): the migration is complete in data and views. Leftovers: a dead 8 px
  `tiles.png` paint fallback in `arena_view.gd` (only for a map with no picture; none shipped),
  the procedural pit atlas drawn at 1x (hand check), three doc lines that say "px" for sim
  units. Not fixed here (cosmetic / doc; open question whether to retire the fallback).
- **major** (3b, feeds Part 2): the sim body `radius` (JSON, 9-13) was never rescaled with the
  2x art and tracks the drawn feet in neither direction: feet_half / radius runs 0.48 (Sno) to
  2.27 (Cerebro); eight of eleven fighters are off by more than 30% (Barkley 1.45, Cerebro 2.27,
  Craglash 1.55, Crease 0.53, Digby 0.61, Hissy 2.09, Pete 0.53, Sno 0.48). Falls and the
  walking guard use only the centre point, so this does not by itself explain a big fighter
  falling through a tiny hole; it does mean a footprint rule must use the sim radius (tunable
  per fighter), not a sprite measure. Radius retune is a balance question → Open questions.

### Part 4: occluded colliders

Details: `docs/qa/part4-occluded.md`. Method: the only layer drawn above fighters is the
`Canopies` group of each map's dressing scene; every wall, fence and brush piece was tested
against the crowns' opaque pixels with the view's geometry.

- **major**, fixed (4092053): Twin Keeps' two mid-field wall pillars (`[40,28,2,8]`,
  `[108,28,2,8]`) were 59% hidden under the crowns of the trees standing on them, two rows
  showing: the reported invisible blockage. Removed from `pieces`, the restyle block that drew
  them as groves removed, the four trees on top of them removed from the dressing scene, tile
  scene rebuilt with `tools/build_tiles.gd -- --set=keeps`. No sim or AI code named them.
- No other map has a wall or fence under a crown. Brush beds under crowns (Twin Keeps 67-82%,
  Greenhouse 52-53%, Caravan Pass 18%) block nothing. Listed, not changed.
- New test `tests/test_occluded_pieces.gd`: no solid piece on any map more than half hidden
  under a canopy (`QA_OCCLUDED=1` prints every piece under a crown).
