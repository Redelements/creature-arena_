Protection: `_respawn` sets `invuln` and `guard_t` to `spawn_protect_seconds` (2 s) in every team mode.
The INITIAL spawn of Versus, Duel, Brawl and Team modes has no invulnerability (owner's decision: none
wanted) and no push-apart; none is needed (below). The sim's `_separate` pass resolves any overlap per tick anyway.
