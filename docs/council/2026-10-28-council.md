## 3. Disagreements left standing

Splits are 2-1 or a champion against the room; nothing here is averaged away.

- **K, team pings (Vex 1, Bastion 5, Marlow 4).** The sharpest split of the council. Bastion:
  team-only, Vision-gated pings with cooldown pips are what make teamwork legible, and on a
  phone with no microphone they are how a team talks. Vex: a "focus" ping puts map awareness
  on the HUD instead of in the player and tells the team where a dive lands; awareness should
  be earned. Marlow sides with Bastion because "shotcall without a mic" is a store-page
  sentence.
- **B, aimed dives (Vex 4, Bastion 1, Marlow 3).** Vex: picking the mage out from behind the
  tank is the skill; the gold tell and the Surge cost keep peel alive. Bastion: it lets a dive
  skip the frontline and deletes peel, the tank's whole job in a 3v3. Marlow: a real highlight
  clip, but a NET bump and it kills mages.
- **A, stealth hidden from guard and assist (Vex 5, Bastion 3, Marlow 2).** Vex: invisible
  should mean invisible, and the assist half needs no NET bump. Bastion accepts the assist half
  and wants the guard half gated on a Normal-bot bench because fighters already lose to
  assassins. Marlow: a day-one fighter main hit from a flank they can't read quits; ship it
  only with a "check behind you" caption in the first fight.
- **F, First Fight 3v3 and the Progression default (Marlow 5, Bastion 3, Vex 2; Progression
  On by default: Marlow yes, Bastion no, Vex no).** Marlow: day one must show the 3v3 pitch
  and end on a reward. Vex: a scripted first match teaches grouping. Both Vex and Bastion say
  flipping Progression to On reverses Kevin's own default and is his call, not the council's.
- **I, the free Demo (Marlow 4, Vex 2, Bastion 2).** Marlow: the front door for wishlists,
  after the icon. Vex: a week of export work and one assassin out of three is a thin taste of
  the dive game. Bastion: L effort that splits attention, and three fighters can't show the
  triangle.
- **D, clip export (Vex 5, Marlow 5, Bastion 2).** Not a dispute on direction: Bastion calls
  it harmless and useful for marketing but says it does nothing for fair fights or clarity.
- **Bastion's slot bench, withdrawn but not conceded.** Bastion still holds that tuning a
  tank on a 1v1 matrix is the wrong yardstick (Craglash "locks foes down while his team does
  the damage"); Vex holds that no assassin gets nerfed off Normal-bot data because a Normal
  CPU never Burrows at 50 px. Any Craglash or assassin balance work should decide this first.
