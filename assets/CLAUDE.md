# assets/ — rules

Loaded automatically when Claude works in this directory. What each folder holds and the art density table:
`assets/README.md`. Licences: `LICENSES.md`. Pipeline: `docs/art-pipeline.md`; sheet specs: `docs/SPRITES.md`.

- Scale: the sim works in units on 8-unit terrain cells and the screen draws 2 px a unit (`ArtScale.PX_PER_UNIT`,
  `docs/plans/32px-migration.md`). Ground and piece tiles are 32 px (16 units), crack/pit overlays 16 px (one cell),
  fighter frames 96–128 px. Don't change the sim's units to match art.
- Fighter sheets are generated by `tools/gen_<id>.py` (own rig, `tools/splat3d.py`, master palette
  `tools/palette.py`). Never hand-edit a generated PNG: change the generator and regenerate (it overwrites the
  sheet), then re-import. PixelLab is not the fighter route (Oct 27 2026, `docs/decisions.md`).
- Terrain comes from the bought Szadi packs, one family per map (`docs/asset-map.md`, recipes in
  `tools/tile_recipes/`); Stadium is RPG Fantasy Worlds Set 1. VFX from kiddolink VFX Complete Set Season 1
  (`assets/vfx/`). Raw packs never enter the repo, only the cut files.
- "Fantasy Lands Warrior" ships only as the stadium crowd (`assets/sprites/crowd.png`); for fighters it is pose
  reference only, never a fighter's art.
- Nothing may resemble Pokémon creatures, names, or trade dress.
