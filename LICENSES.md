# Licences and credits

Ready 4 Rambles is made by Kevin (Redelements). Most of its art and all of its sound are
generated by the scripts in `tools/` (`gen_*.py`, `gen_audio.py`, `splat3d.py`...) and are
part of this repository. A few files are not: they are bought art, cut down to the pieces
the game uses, plus a free font and the engine. This file lists them. In the game, the same
credits are under **Settings > Credits** (`src/ui/credits_screen.gd`), with Godot's licence
and its third-party notices.

**This repository is public. The files listed under "Szadi art" and "kiddolink" below are
not covered by any licence or permission this repository grants and may not be reused,
copied out or redistributed.** They are here only so the game builds. If you fork or copy
this repository, leave them out or buy your own licence from the artist.

The raw packs never enter this repository: only the cut, edited pieces the game uses do (no
complete sheets, no source PSDs). The cutting scripts read a local copy of the packs from a
path passed on the command line; the game and CI use the committed cuts alone.

**Pending:** Kevin has asked both artists (Szadi art and kiddolink) for a written OK to keep
these cuts in a public repository. Until both answer yes, treat the cuts as provisional: if
either says no, the cuts come out of the public repo (kept in a private asset store, pulled
in at build time).

The itch.io pages carry the content tag "No generative AI was used": the artist's statement
about how the packs were made, not a term of the licence. The licence says nothing about
generative tools either way; to stay clearly inside "edit and cut for your game", the
PixelLab spec (`docs/pixellab-spec.md`) keeps Szadi art out of any upload: the Warrior is a
pose reference read by eye, never fed to the tool.

Because of (d): **the game's logo, icon, and store and marketing art stay free of bought
art.** The wordmark (`assets/ui/logo.png`) is generated by `tools/gen_logo.py`; keep it and
any future icon or store art that way.

Files from it (cut by `tools/cut_tiles.py` from `tools/tile_recipes/set1.json`, colours
snapped to the game's palette):

- `assets/tiles/set1_ground.png`, `assets/tiles/set1_pieces.png`,
  `assets/tiles/set1_overlay.png`: ground (dirt, grass, cobbles, water), piece (boulders,
  tall grass) and crack / pit tiles cut from Set 1 (the overlay mixes Set 1 cliff strips with
  generated cracks).
- `assets/tiles/set1_decor_*.png` (trees, a bush) and `assets/tiles/set1_anim_waterfall.png`.
- `assets/tiles/set1_manifest.json`: where the cuts landed.
- `assets/tiles/set1_*.tres`: the Godot TileSets `tools/build_tiles.gd` makes from them.
- `assets/arenas/<id>.tscn`: each tiled arena's ground, painted with those tiles.

Files from **RPG Worlds GrassLand v2** (`RPGW_GrassLand_v2.01`) and **RPG Worlds Decorative
Props** (`RPGW_DecorativeProps_v1.0`), both in the same bundle, with Set 1 (cut by
`tools/cut_tiles.py` from `tools/tile_recipes/grassland.json`, colours snapped to the palette):

- `assets/tiles/grassland_ground.png`: the Greenhouse's turf (Set 1), GrassLand's moss kerb,
  flower meadow and its own turf, Set 1's grass and water blobs.
- `assets/tiles/grassland_pieces.png`: Set 1 boulders and tall grass, Decorative Props fences.
- `assets/tiles/grassland_overlay.png`: GrassLand cliff strips and a cave dark for the pit
  walls and floor, with generated cracks and edges.
- `assets/tiles/grassland_decor_*.png`: Decorative Props pots, barrels and crates, Set 1's
  birch and bush.
- `assets/tiles/grassland_manifest.json`, `assets/tiles/grassland_*.tres` and
  `assets/arenas/greenhouse.tscn`: derived from the above.

Fantasy Lands Warrior: nothing from it is in the repository yet. Add its files here when
they are.

The MIT licence asks that its copyright notice and permission notice ship with every copy:
that includes every exported build of the game. Godot is also built from third-party
libraries (FreeType, HarfBuzz, ENet, mbedTLS, zlib and more), each with its own notice. The

licence) followed by every licence text in `Engine.get_license_info()`.
