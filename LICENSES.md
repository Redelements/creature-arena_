# Licences and credits

Ready 4 Rambles is made by Kevin (Redelements). Most of its art and all of its sound are
generated by the scripts in `tools/` (`gen_*.py`, `gen_audio.py`, `splat3d.py`...) and are
part of this repository. A few files are not: they are bought art, cut down to the pieces
the game uses, plus a free font and the engine. This file lists them. In the game, the same
credits are under **Settings > Credits** (`src/ui/credits_screen.gd`), with Godot's licence
and its third-party notices.

**This repository is public. The files listed under "Szadi art" and "kiddolink" below are
not covered by any licence or permission this repository grants and may not be reused,
copied out or redistributed.** They are here only so the game builds. If you fork or copy
this repository, leave them out or buy your own licence from the artist.

The raw packs never enter this repository: only the cut, edited pieces the game uses do (no
complete sheets, no source PSDs). The cutting scripts read a local copy of the packs from a
path passed on the command line; the game and CI use the committed cuts alone.

**Pending:** Kevin has asked both artists (Szadi art and kiddolink) for a written OK to keep
these cuts in a public repository. Until both answer yes, treat the cuts as provisional: if
either says no, the cuts come out of the public repo (kept in a private asset store, pulled
in at build time).
