# Third-party assets

Most of Ready 4 Rambles' art and all of its sound are generated by the scripts in `tools/`
(`gen_*.py`, `gen_audio.py`) and fall under this repository's own terms. The files below
don't: they are bought art, cut down to the pieces the game uses.

**The files listed under "Szadi art" and "kiddolink" are not covered by any licence of this
repository and may not be reused or redistributed.** They are here only so the game builds.
If you fork or copy this repository, leave them out or buy your own licence.

The whole packs never enter this repository: only the cut, edited pieces the game uses do
(no complete sheets, no source files). `tools/cut_packs.py` makes the cuts from a local copy
of the packs whose path is passed in; the game and CI use the committed cuts alone.

## Szadi art: RPG Worlds Set 1 (RPGFW_Set1)

<https://szadiart.itch.io/> (part of Szadi art's RPG bundle, bought October 2026).

Licence in brief (the pack's `license.txt`): commercial use allowed; editing, modifying and
cutting allowed; selling works made with the assets allowed. Not allowed: reselling the assets,
original or modified; using them in a logo, trademark or service mark. Credit is optional.

Files from it:

- `tools/art_src/set1_twin_keeps.png`: the swatch (two grass tiles, an earth tile, a cobble
  tile, six tall-grass tufts), cut by `tools/cut_packs.py`. Not shipped in builds (Godot
  ignores `tools/`).
- `assets/sprites/arena_twin_keeps.png`, `arena_twin_keeps_cracked.png`,
  `arena_twin_keeps_alt.png`, `arena_twin_keeps_far.png`: the Twin Keeps pictures baked by
  `tools/gen_arenas.py`, whose floor is painted from that swatch (recoloured onto the game's
  palette and combined with generated art).

## kiddolink: VFX Complete Set, Season 1

<https://kiddolink.itch.io/vfx-complete-set-season-1-pixel-art> (bought October 2026).

Licence in brief (the itch.io page; the download has no licence file): commercial use
allowed; editing allowed. Not allowed: reselling or redistributing the pack.

Files from it (single rows of frames, cropped, colours snapped to the game's palette):

- `assets/sprites/vfx/impact_burst.png` (vfx_hit_impact)
- `assets/sprites/vfx/slash_claws.png`, `assets/sprites/vfx/slash_arc.png` (vfx_slash)
- `assets/sprites/vfx/spark_star.png` (vfx_spark)

## Free assets

- `assets/fonts/m5x7.ttf`: the m5x7 pixel font by Daniel Linssen, CC0 (public domain;
  `assets/fonts/m5x7-LICENSE.txt`).

## Generated

Everything else under `assets/` (fighters, arenas, crowd, effects, sounds, music) is made by
the generators in `tools/` and is part of this repository.
